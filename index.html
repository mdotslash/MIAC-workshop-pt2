The Opportunity

Building Artist-Powered Digital Collections & Cultural Archives

Join AMPL Labs in Santa Fe, New Mexico for a digital cultural heritage professional development intensive for artists interested in working with communities and cultural institutions to preserve and protect cultural knowledge.

The intensive will focus on using accessible 3D scanning technology and open-source digital collections as medium for restorative infrastructure and innovative cultural collaboration.

Artists will also learn how to effectively collaborate with institutions to ensure community heritage, intellectual property is not just "stored" but thrives through active participation and agency.

This training is offered by award-winning artist Mayowa Tomori (AMPL Labs) and Indigenous creative technologist Monica Silva Lovato.

Training Focus

  • Technical Mastery of 3D Scanning: Learn how to create high-fidelity 3D models using photogrammetry.
  • Museum Technology Integration: Learn how to create mixed reality 3D archives using Smithsonian Voyager and other Open Source tools.
  • Ethics & Working with Institutions: How to minimize harm, take care of your team, community while working with collections.
  • Business Strategy: How to build sustainable business partnerships with cultural institution, fund collaborative research.
  • Activating Collections: Learn how to adopt archival research into your creative practice.

Research Case Study

Casting Memories: Smithsonian x AMPL Labs

In 2022, AMPL Labs led an award-winning collaboration with the Smithsonian Institution and Mozilla Foundation to animate their digital collections using new forms of storytelling and emerging technology.

Cultural Technology for The People

We defined an accessible 3D-scanning, archiving methodology that moves away from extractive tech and toward restorative infrastructure.

Decolonizing the Archive

We led a reparative process that invited artists, technologists from impacted communities to participate in the co-design, co-creation, or destruction of archives held by culural institutions.

Innovative Possibilities (I.P.)

We developed new models for ownership, IP and licensing that make it easier for artists, program designers, researchers to expand the impact of their work into their communities.
