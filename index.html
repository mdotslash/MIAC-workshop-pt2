The Opportunity

Building Artist-Powered Digital Collections & Cultural Archives

Join AMPL Labs for a digital cultural heritage professional development intensive for artists interested in working with communities and cultural institutions.

The intensive will focus on using accessible 3D scanning technology and open-source digital collections to preserve and protect cultural knowledge and explore emergent technology being used for innovative cultural collaborations.

Training Focus

This training is offered by award-winning artist Mayowa Tomori (AMPL Labs) and Indigenous creative technologist Monica Silva Lovato and will cover technical and creative content, including:


  • Hands-On 3D Scanning: Step-by-step training on creating and publishing high-fidelity 3D models for archives.
  • Open-Source Museum Technology: Tips for integrating 3D objects files and metadata into your databases using Smithsonian Voyager and other open-source tools.
  • Activating Collections: Creative tips for artists, collectives looking to adopt and activate archival content for their creative practices.
  • Ethics & Collaborating with Institutions: Practical guides for minimizing harm, take care of your team, and vulnerable communites while working with sensitive collections.
  • Funding Strategy: How to build sustainable business partnerships with cultural institutions, and fund collaborative research using public and private sources.
The Training

Workshop Curriculum

The workshop will give you the technical skills for leading 3D and XR archival research and creative projects in cultural institutions. We can adapt the curriculum to fit your unique use-case or client's needs.

01: Foundations

XR in Museums

This module covers use-cases for 3D/AR in cultural institutions and includes a hands-on photogrammetry intensive with a partner cultural institution or private archivist.

02: Implementation

Digital Publishing

Learn how to create interactive, web-based 3D & AR experiences that are mobile-friendly and accessible. We also show you how to integrate 3D content into games, apps and New Media applications.

03: Innovation

Regenerative Commons

We'll explore emergent models for Community Intellectual Property and ways to scale your visionary ideas for programming and massive community impact.

Research Case Study

Casting Memories:
Smithsonian x AMPL Labs

In 2022, AMPL Labs led an award-winning collaboration with the Smithsonian Institution and Mozilla Foundation to animate their digital collections using new forms of storytelling and emerging technology.

Cultural Technology for The People

We defined an accessible 3D-scanning, archiving methodology that moves away from extractive tech and toward restorative infrastructure.

Decolonizing the Archive

We led a reparative process that invited artists, technologists from impacted communities to participate in the co-design, co-creation, or destruction of archives held by cultural institutions.

Innovative Possibilities (I.P.)

We developed new models for ownership, IP and licensing that make it easier for artists, program designers, researchers to expand the impact of their work into their communities.
